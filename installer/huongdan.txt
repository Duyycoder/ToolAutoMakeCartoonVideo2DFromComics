AutoCartoon Video Maker - He thong tu dong chuyen truyen chu thanh video hoat hinh 2D co long tieng.

YEU CAU HE THONG
  - Windows 10/11 64-bit
  - Ket noi Internet (BAT BUOC trong luc cai dat)
  - Trong 20-30 GB dung luong o dia
  - GPU NVIDIA >= 6GB VRAM (khuyen nghi; khong co van chay duoc bang CPU nhung cham)
  - KHONG can cai san Python hay Git - bo cai se tu lo het

QUA TRINH CAI DAT GOM 2 GIAI DOAN
  1. Chep ma nguon vao may (nhanh, vai giay)
  2. Cai moi truong AI: tu dong tai Python 3.11, tao moi truong ao,
     tai thu vien va model AI. Buoc nay hien cua so den (console) de ban
     theo doi tien do, can Internet va co the mat 30-60 phut tuy toc do mang.

SAU KHI CAI XONG
  - Mo ung dung bang bieu tuong "AutoCartoon Video Maker" ngoai Desktop
    hoac trong Start Menu.
  - Neu dung engine dich "Gemini API (Offline/Local)": dien cookies vao
    file toolCaoTruyen\Gemini-API\cookies.json (xem README trong thu muc do).
  - Muon cai lai moi truong: chay "AutoCartoon Video Maker - Cai dat moi truong"
    trong Start Menu.
