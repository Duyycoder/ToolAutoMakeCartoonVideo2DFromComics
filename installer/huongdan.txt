Cao va Dich Video - cao video tu link, tao phu de (Whisper/OCR), dich, long tieng
va dung phim ngay tren may.

YEU CAU HE THONG
  - Windows 10/11 64-bit
  - Ket noi Internet (BAT BUOC trong luc cai dat)
  - Trong 20-30 GB dung luong o dia
  - GPU NVIDIA >= 6GB VRAM (khuyen nghi; khong co van chay duoc bang CPU nhung cham)
  - KHONG can cai san Python hay Git - bo cai se tu lo het

QUA TRINH CAI DAT GOM 2 GIAI DOAN
  1. Chep ma nguon vao may (nhanh, vai giay)
  2. Cai moi truong AI: tu dong tai Python 3.11, tao moi truong ao,
     tai thu vien va model AI. Buoc nay hien cua so den (console) de ban
     theo doi tien do, can Internet va co the mat 30-60 phut tuy toc do mang.

CAP NHAT LEN BAN MOI
  - Chi can chay bo cai ban moi tren may da cai. Bo cai tu nhan ra thu muc cu,
    chi con nut Install: du an, cau hinh, API key, thu vien va model AI da tai
    duoc giu nguyen, chi tai them thu vien nao ban moi can (vai phut).
  - Nho tat ung dung truoc khi cap nhat (bo cai se nhac neu app dang mo).
  - Phien ban dang dung hien canh ten ung dung o trang chu (vd "v2.0.5").

SAU KHI CAI XONG
  - Mo ung dung bang bieu tuong "Cao va Dich Video" ngoai Desktop
    hoac trong Start Menu.
  - Engine dich mac dinh la Ollama (chay tren may, khong can API key).
    Chon thanh phan "Dich offline" khi cai de tu tai Ollama + model dich hy-mt2.
  - Muon cai lai moi truong: chay "Cao va Dich Video - Cai dat moi truong"
    trong Start Menu.
